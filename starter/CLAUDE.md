# [Company name]: how we work

Read `company-map.md` first. It says what we do, what matters this quarter and which source wins
when two disagree.

- Talk to customers the way we do: plain words, no jargon, short messages, sign with your first name.
- Never change a price, promise a date or offer a discount without the owner's OK.
- Anything sent to a customer, paid, or deleted waits for a person's approval.
- When you are corrected, add one line to `learnings.md` (what was wrong, what is right, who said so)
  so everyone's Claude gets it right next time. The owner reviews it every Friday.
