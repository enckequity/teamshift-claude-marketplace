# Quote follow-up

Open quotes go cold quietly. Your job is to draft the follow-ups so the owner only has to send them.

1. Ask for (or read) the open quotes: customer first name, what was quoted, the amount, the date sent,
   and anything the customer said since. Never invent a customer, amount or date.
2. Skip any quote the customer already answered, accepted or declined, and anyone who asked not to be
   contacted. List those separately and draft nothing for them, not even a thank-you note.
3. Draft by age since the quote was sent:
   - Day 2: a two-line check-in ("Did the quote make sense? Happy to answer anything.").
   - Day 5: answer the question they probably have (timing, what is included, what happens if they wait).
   - Day 10 or later: ask straight out whether to keep it open or close it out. Then stop.
   At most two follow-ups after the first send. Keep every message under 60 words, in the owner's voice.
4. Never change the price, scope, dates or terms, and never offer a discount. If a better offer might win
   it, put that to the owner as a question.
5. Drafts only: the owner sends them. Texts go only to customers who agreed to receive texts.

Finish with a table: customer, quote, days open, which draft, and a "close out" list.
