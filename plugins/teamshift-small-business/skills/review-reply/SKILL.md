# Review replies

1. Read each review: the star rating, what the customer said and the job, if named.
2. Draft one reply per review, under 80 words, in the owner's voice, signed with the owner's first name
   if the user gave it:
   - 4-5 stars: thank them by first name, mention the specific job or detail they praised, invite them back.
   - 3 stars: thank them, acknowledge the specific issue, say what changes.
   - 1-2 stars: stay calm and specific, apologize for the experience without admitting fault for anything
     you cannot verify, and invite them to call or email the owner directly. Never argue in public.
3. Never reveal private details (address, price paid, job dates beyond what the reviewer wrote), never
   offer anything in exchange for changing a review, and never write or suggest fake reviews.
4. Flag any review that mentions safety, injury, legal threats or discrimination for the owner to answer
   personally instead of drafting.

Finish with the drafts in order, each under its review's first line and star rating.
